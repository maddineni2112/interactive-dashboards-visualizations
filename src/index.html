Credit & Loan Data: A Visual Exploration

Interactive visualizations analyzing credit scores, income levels, loan amounts, and approval decisions using multiple chart types from your loan approval dataset.

Scatter & Bubble Plots

Ploting the scatterplots using x-y encoding how income and credit score and loan amount (bubble size) relate to loan approval. Both the original and optimized scatter views are also included so as to compare them better.

Stacked & Grouped Bar Charts

Examine how loan approvals Whether approved or rejected have been trending by the different income groups by displaying the contribution of various employment years groups within each income group in stacked and grouped bar charts.

Stacked Area Chart

As an connection with the relationship between total income, and credit-score ranges, each employmed years(experience) segment is overlaid to demonstrate the role of long-term work experience in overall income patterns.

Line Charts

Tracking the average loan amount requested by the people across credit-score ranges. The first chart shows extreme overplotting with individual years, while the optimized comparative chart reveals clearer patterns using grouped employment categories.
